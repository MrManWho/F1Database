{{ who }}

What's stored

  • Your username, display name, a salted password hash and, if you add one, your email address.
  • For each league you join: your role, the driver you control, your notification choices and what you did (the league's activity log).
  • A list of your signed-in devices (browser name and when it was last used) so you can sign them out.

What isn't

  • Screenshots you import are read in your own browser. They're never uploaded, stored or sent to any AI or paid text-recognition service.
  • No advertising, tracking or analytics services.
  • Nobody else sees your email address. League emails are sent only for the categories you choose, per league.

How long it's kept

  • Your login is kept until you delete it (My account) or ask the site owner to. Deleting it removes the login straight away.
  • League results, standings and activity logs stay with the league so everyone else's history stays correct. They're kept until the league's Race Master deletes the league.
  • League backups (scheduled, and safety copies made before big changes) stay on the server until a Race Master or the site owner deletes them.
  • Demo leagues and demo guest logins are removed automatically after a couple of hours.

Public leagues

A league's Race Master can make it public. Public pages show completed results, standings and driver names only: never emails, usernames, private notes, drafts or rounds still in progress.

Your choices

From your account you can download everything your account holds, sign out devices, pause every email, leave a league or delete your account.

Using the site

  • Paddock Legacy is a free tool for tracking fan-run racing game leagues. It isn't affiliated with Formula 1, the FIA or any game publisher.
  • Be decent to the people in your league. Don't post anything hateful, harassing or illegal in league names, descriptions, news or comments.
  • Public leagues can be reported. The site owner may remove a league from the directory, or remove logins that break these terms.
  • Keep your password to yourself. Turn on two-step sign-in if you run a league.

Your league data

Race Masters are responsible for their league's data. Download a backup from the league's Backups page regularly. The site is provided as-is, free of charge and without guarantees; it may change or go offline.

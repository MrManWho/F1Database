All logins

Race Master: full admin of every league. Manages logins, saves and backups, and can see every garage, offer and negotiation. A Race Master only drives if you link them to a player driver in a league.

Member: a normal login. What they can do in each league (Race Master, Scorekeeper, Member or Spectator, and which driver they control) is set in one place: that league's Players & Logins page. There's no separate Scorekeeper switch here any more.

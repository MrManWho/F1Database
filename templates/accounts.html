Encrypted backup to keep somewhere else

The automatic backups are stored on the same disk as the site, so they can't help if the disk or the hosting account is lost. Download one encrypted file with every league and all accounts and keep it somewhere else: your computer, a USB stick or a cloud drive. It's locked with a passphrase only you know. Without the passphrase nobody, including you, can open it, so keep it in a password manager.

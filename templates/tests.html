How a race night from the game works

  1. During the race night the recorder on your PC sends each session to the league as it ends. In the test league, qualifying has already arrived.
  2. When the race ends the recorder sends the race. Here, Send the race from the game does that part: the same made-up race goes through exactly the same steps as a real one.
  3. The round fills itself in: qualifying and race order, the retirement, the fastest lap, the AI level, the weather, the winner's race time and both players' times against their AI teammates. Only blanks are filled.
  4. Everyone gets an alert (in the bell; test leagues send no emails or phone alerts) that the results are in from the game.
  5. Open the round: Review & submit shows what was filled in and anything left for you. Press Approve and submit and the weekend is submitted exactly like Submit weekend.

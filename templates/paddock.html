Car ratings

Set these to match the game's car performance

Car ratings decide which cars count as fast before three rounds of this season are done. That affects the bar teams set in the transfer market and the "vs the car" part of the difficulty score. Every winter the cars develop on their own: they drift toward the pack, better finishers get more money, and there's some luck. You can always correct them here.

Recalculate Reputation history

Form is always worked out fresh from results, so formula changes in an update apply to every season straight away. Reputation from finished seasons is locked when a new season starts. Press this after an update that changes the maths (for example v1.7's qualifying-vs-finish racecraft) to replay every season with the current formula. A backup is made first.

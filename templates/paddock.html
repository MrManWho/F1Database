Car ratings

Set these to match the game's car performance

Car ratings decide which cars count as fast before three rounds of this season are done. That affects the bar teams set in the transfer market and the "vs the car" part of the difficulty score. Every winter the cars develop on their own: they drift toward the pack, better constructors' finishers develop faster, and there's some luck. You can always correct them here.

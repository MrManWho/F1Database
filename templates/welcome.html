Create a league

A short setup asks for the name, season, drivers and which career systems you want. A simple results tracker, a multiplayer career league or the full simulation. Everything can be changed later.

Join a league

Accept an invitation from your League Library, or ask to join a league that takes requests. The Race Master approves each person and their role.

Follow a public league

Public leagues share standings, results, driver pages and records with anyone, no account needed. Nothing private is ever public.

Roles

Race Master
Runs the league: calendar, grid, contracts, members, settings, and corrections to completed rounds.
Scorekeeper
Enters and submits results for rounds that aren't complete yet. Nothing administrative.
Driver / Member
Follows everything and, with a driver, manages their own career: offers, pledges, press and targets.
Spectator
Read-only. Perfect for fans and friends.

Privacy

Leagues are private unless the Race Master makes them public, and a league is only listed in the directory by choice. Email addresses, notes, drafts and settings are never public.

Notifications

You choose what each league may email or alert you about, league by league. Joining one league never subscribes you to another, and every email links to that league's settings.

Free, and local where it matters

Paddock Legacy has no paid APIs or per-use charges. Screenshot import reads results right in your browser: screenshots are never uploaded or sent to an AI provider.

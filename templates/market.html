Offers & windows

Opening a window has every team judge each player driver and send offers (drivers still under contract for that season are left alone). Before the first round they're for this season; after that they're for next season. The Rookie Draft opens by itself when a league starts as rookies, and Silly Season opens by itself once half the calendar is complete. Players accept offers from their own login.

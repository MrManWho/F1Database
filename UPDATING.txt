HOW TO UPDATE F1 UNIVERSE TRACKER
=================================

Your careers, logins and passwords are NOT stored in this folder. They live in
%LOCALAPPDATA%\F1UniverseTracker, so replacing the program files never touches them.

1. Close the black tracker window (this stops the website for a few seconds).
2. Download the new version ZIP and extract it.
3. Drag everything from the new folder into this folder and choose
   "Replace the files in the destination".
   (Keep the .venv folder that's already here - the ZIP doesn't include one.)
4. Double-click run_lan.bat (or run.bat) again.

That's it. Anyone who was logged in stays logged in - they may just need to
refresh the page. If an update changes the save format, a backup of each
career is made automatically in %LOCALAPPDATA%\F1UniverseTracker\backups
the first time it's opened, before anything is changed.

Extra safety: before big updates, use Career & Saves > Download backup.
